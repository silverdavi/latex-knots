\subsection*{Crossing changes}
The same projection can be reused while selected crossings are switched:
\[
  \kd{trefoil}
  \longrightarrow
  \kd[change=2]{trefoil},
  \qquad
  \kd{figureeight}
  \longrightarrow
  \kd[change=2]{figureeight}.
\]

\subsection*{Local skein triple}
\[
  \kd{L+}
  \qquad
  \kd{L-}
  \qquad
  \kd{L0}
  \qquad\qquad
  \skeinrel.
\]

\subsection*{Custom strands and colour}
Diagrams can be declared from arbitrary TikZ paths, with per-strand
styling:
\[
  \kd{colouredhopf}.
\]
